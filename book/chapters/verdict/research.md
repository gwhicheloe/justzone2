## Numbers used in the draft
- Mølmen 2025: 353 studies; mitochondrial content +23% continuous, +27% intervals; sprints almost four times as much per hour (Introduction).
- Tønnessen 2014: 1.3 hours a week above the first threshold; 91% of endurance time below it.
- Seiler 2013: 4 × 8 11.4% vs 4 × 4 5.5%, about six hours a week (doubled).
- Hard dose 40 minutes to an hour a week (How many; my inference).
- George's winter: four or five sessions a week, 45 to 60 minutes, about 142 bpm; three to five hours a week; before it three or four hours of mostly hard riding, irregular (Introduction; What easy riding does). Sa Calobra: two minutes eleven seconds faster (Introduction).
- Arem 2015: 31% lower risk at one to two times the minimum, 39% at three to five times (Beyond performance).
- Carbohydrate: about 60 g an hour gets most of the benefit (Smith 2013; Fuelling).

- Buist 2008: 20.8% vs 20.3% injured; 13-week graded vs 8-week standard (The training week).
- Copying the ratio: a rider on seven hours at 80% easy has about 1.4 h of hard training [Own: 7 × 0.2 = 1.4], close to the champions' 1.3 h.
- Strength: Llanos-Lagos 2026, effects in efforts of a few minutes to an hour (Strength training). Heat: Mikkelsen 2019, Copenhagen amateurs no faster than controls (Heat training).
- Lactate clearance: What easy riding does, grade C: San-Millán 2018 cross-sectional; Evertsen 2001 [Abs], 20 elite skiers, MCT1 fell 12% after five months of mostly easy training, unchanged in the threshold group.
