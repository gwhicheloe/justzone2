## The question
How can you tell you're getting fitter without an FTP test?

## The argument (to approve)
1. **The position, first paragraph:** measure something repeatable weekly (power at a fixed heart rate on a standard ride) and judge it by the monthly average; that beats any single test, a watch's VO₂max or a fitness-and-fatigue chart. Grade C for the routine.
2. Power at a fixed heart rate: Buchheit 2014; the Lamberts test (Lamberts 2011, 2024); a home version, untested. Day-to-day heart rate noise (Lamberts 2004).
3. Signal and noise: simulated figure (true +5% over 12 weeks, 3% error; the last single reading is below the first).
4. Best efforts from ordinary riding (Karsten 2015).
5. Drift on long rides (Durability's test; Smyth 2022). Grade B/C.
6. How you feel (Saw 2016). Grade B.
7. Heart-rate variability: *One study, up close*: Javaloyes 2019 (trial table): within-group significance and magnitude-based inference, no between-group difference; Manresa-Rocamora 2021 meta-analysis: small at most. Grade B.
8. Watch VO₂max (Molina-Garcia 2022, figure). Grade A that the individual error is large.
9. *Reading the research: a model is not a measurement*: Banister 1975; Hellard 2006 (figure of parameter uncertainty).
10. Detraining (Mujika & Padilla 2000 I and II; Coyle 1984 in Chapter 2); George's illness experience from Chapter 2; how to return.
11. The routine: three numbers and a note.
