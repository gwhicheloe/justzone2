Two aims

Training advice goes through fashions, and Zone 2 is the latest. The book is meant to help readers cut through the noise, so every chapter does two things.

Explain the mechanism

The biology and physiology a training claim rests on, so readers understand why an effect should happen, not only that a study found one. How bodies adapt to training lays the groundwork and later chapters refer back to it.

Teach readers to judge research

Study design, sample size, effect sizes, and the difference between a plausible mechanism and a demonstrated effect. How to read a training study sets out the toolkit, and every chapter then practises one skill on a real study.

The spine

Every chapter hangs off one model of endurance performance, which the book calls the engine. Each training question becomes: which of these four does it change, and how good is the evidence?

sustainable power ≈ VO₂max × fraction you can sustain × efficiency, then ask how much survives three hours (durability)

Running through every chapter

How it works

Each chapter explains the mechanism before the evidence: what's happening in the muscle, heart or blood, with speculation labelled as speculation.

Reading the research

A short box per chapter that practises one skill on one of its own studies. In Fuelling: the fastest Ironman athletes eat the most, so does eating more make you fast, or does being fast let you eat?

Evidence grades

Each major claim gets a grade, so a reader can see at a glance how much weight it carries.

ASeveral randomised trials or a solid meta-analysis BConsistent, but small studies or a single lab CObservational, mechanistic or expert opinion

One study, up close

A box per chapter that takes one key paper apart: who was studied, for how long, what changed, and what it can't tell us.

Charts redrawn from the data

Figures built from published numbers, in black and white for print, each titled with the point it makes. No figures copied from papers.

From the saddle

Your rides, mistakes and opinions, in your words. Several drafted chapters still have a placeholder waiting for one.

  • Back matter: glossary, a session library (each session with its evidence grade), and references by chapter.
  • App mentions: the introduction and a closing page only.
  • House style: British spelling, watts and W/kg, every number traceable to a source.
