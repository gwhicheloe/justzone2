# Studies that don't serve their chapter's argument

17 October 2026, after George asked: "can you double check for studies introduced that don't add to the core narrative of a chapter". Every chapter's citations were mapped. Chapters 5 to 10, 12, 13 and 15 were read in full, and the rest were read in their paragraphs that cite a source used only once. Nothing below has been changed yet.

The test for each study was whether the chapter's conclusion, or a piece of practical advice, would be weaker without it. Studies that are the only support for a claim were kept, however minor. Chapter 11 was restructured yesterday and is not repeated here. The Drugs chapter was left out because of its legal read.
