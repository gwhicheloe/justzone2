# Zone 2 made him faster.
# Just not for the reason
# he’d been told.

After a winter of easy rides on a smart trainer, George Whicheloe took more than two minutes off his best time up Sa Calobra and told readers of his website why: easy riding was the single most effective way to build mitochondria. Then he read the research. The largest review of the question, 353 training studies, found he was wrong.

*Just Zone 2?* is what he found when he kept reading. Written for cyclists on six to ten hours a week who would rather understand their training than just follow it, it explains the biology behind each popular claim, then grades the evidence for it A, B or C.

- What easy riding does to the body, and what it doesn’t
- Why champions ride mostly easy, and what not to copy
- Finding your own Zone 2 without a formula
- Which intervals did best in the trials, and how many a week
- Fuelling, strength, heat and drugs: what else gets the credit
- How to read a training study for yourself

When the next fashion arrives, and it will, you’ll know what to ask.
