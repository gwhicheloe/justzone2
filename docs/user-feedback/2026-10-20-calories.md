# User report: calories missing in Apple Fitness (20 October 2026)

**Status:** fixed in code (1.0.5, not yet released). Reply **not sent yet**: send once 1.0.5 is on the App Store. The rider must also switch on Active Energy under Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → JustZone2 (found by George testing build 26).

## The report (summary)

A user on 1.0.4 (smart trainer: **Zwift Hub**; heart rate: **AirPods Pro 3**) found calories missing or wrong in Apple Fitness after JustZone2 workouts, which skewed their workout summaries and activity tracking.

## Cause

JustZone2 had permission to write Active Energy but never wrote it. Workouts reached Apple Health with heart rate and power only. With AirPods, the live HealthKit data source may have added an iPhone estimate from heart rate, hence "missing or incorrect".

## Fix (1.0.5)

Active energy is written every second from measured power (kJ of work ≈ kcal burned in total), minus a typical resting burn of 70 kcal/h, since Apple Health counts resting energy separately: 1 h at 160 W ≈ 506 active kcal. The Strava upload keeps the total. The live data source's own active-energy estimate is disabled in AirPods mode to avoid double counting. Past workouts can't be corrected.

**Verify on a real ride before replying:** the workout in the Fitness app should show active calories close to (average watts × 3.6 − 70) × hours.

## Draft reply (send once 1.0.5 is on the App Store)

> Thanks for taking the time to report this, and for the detail. You were right: JustZone2 wasn't saving calories to Apple Health at all, so your workouts reached Apple Fitness without them, or with only the iPhone's estimate from your AirPods heart rate.
>
> Version 1.0.5, out now, fixes this. It saves calories calculated from your trainer's measured power, which is more accurate for cycling than an estimate from heart rate, so they'll show in Apple Fitness and count towards your Move ring.
>
> There's one setting to switch on first. iPhone keeps Health permissions per app, so it doesn't turn this one on by itself after the update:
>
> 1. Open the Settings app.
> 2. Go to Privacy & Security, then Health, then JustZone2.
> 3. Under "Allow JustZone2 to Write", turn on Active Energy.
>
> Your next ride should then show its calories in Fitness. Unfortunately the update can't add them to workouts you've already saved.
>
> Also, good to hear the Zwift Hub works with it. Is ERG mode holding your target power properly?

## Also learned
