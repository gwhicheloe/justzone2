# User report: calories missing in Apple Fitness (20 October 2026)

**Status:** fixed in code (1.0.5, not yet released). Reply **not sent yet**: send once 1.0.5 is on the App Store.

## The report (summary)

A user on 1.0.4 (smart trainer: **Zwift Hub**; heart rate: **AirPods Pro 3**) found calories missing or wrong in Apple Fitness after JustZone2 workouts, which skewed their workout summaries and activity tracking.

## Cause

JustZone2 had permission to write Active Energy but never wrote it. Workouts reached Apple Health with heart rate and power only. With AirPods, the live HealthKit data source may have added an iPhone estimate from heart rate, hence "missing or incorrect".

## Fix (1.0.5)

Active energy is written every second from measured power (kJ of work ≈ kcal burned; 1 h at 160 W ≈ 576 kcal), the same rule as the Strava upload. The live data source's own active-energy estimate is disabled in AirPods mode to avoid double counting. Past workouts can't be corrected.

**Verify on a real ride before replying:** the workout in the Fitness app should show active calories close to average watts × hours × 3.6.
