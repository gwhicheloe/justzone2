Where the time goes

    How this week is built

    The rules come from our review of the Zone 2 research. In short:

    • Two hard sessions, every week. One VO₂max session and one threshold session, on non-consecutive days. At low volume these give you the most per hour, so they're protected first.
    • Everything else is genuinely easy. Extra hours go into Zone 2, not more intensity. That's why the easy share grows as your hours grow: a big week has to be mostly easy to be absorbable.
    • The long ride is optional. It builds durability, the ability to keep producing power late in a long ride. That matters for long events and much less for general fitness. It only goes in once there's room for it alongside the two hard sessions.
    • At least one full rest day. Two when your week is six hours or less.
    • Avoid the grey zone. Easy riding done a bit too hard costs you tempo-level fatigue without tempo-level benefit. Zone 2 only works if it's actually Zone 2.

    Common questions

    How many hours of Zone 2 should I do a week?

    As many as your week allows once the two hard sessions are in. At four hours, Zone 2 fills the gaps and helps you recover. At ten or more, it's most of your riding, because that much training only works if most of it is easy.

    Should I still do intervals if I only have a few hours?

    Yes. With little time, the two hard sessions give you the most for each hour, so they come first. Zone 2 fills the rest.

    Why only two hard sessions?

    Two or three hard sessions a week, with everything else genuinely easy, is the pattern most well-trained endurance athletes settle on. Two keeps the hard days hard and leaves room to recover.

    Do I need the long ride?

    Only if you're preparing for long events. If your goal is general fitness, switch it off and the same hours are spread across shorter Zone 2 rides.

    I'm new to structured training. Where do I start?

    Start with fewer hours than you think you can manage, and consider riding Thursday as Zone 2 for the first few weeks so there's only one hard session. Add hours gradually, and take an easier week every three or four weeks.
